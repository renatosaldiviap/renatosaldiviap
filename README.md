### 🚀 Featured Analytics & BI Projects

#### 📦 [Apex Logistics: Operational Risk & Churn Analytics (GCP + Power BI)](https://github.com/renatosaldiviap/PowerBI_GCP_Logistics_Churn)
* **Problem:** Evaluate how delivery delays impact customer churn and Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) across B2B segments.
* **Methodology & Architecture:** Designed an end-to-end cloud pipeline using `Google Cloud Storage` and `BigQuery (SQL)` structured under a Kimball dimensional model (`Star Schema`), connected to `Power BI` with custom `DAX` measures.
* **Outcome:** Identified that average delays exceeding 45 minutes resulted in a 100% cancellation rate among Enterprise accounts, pinpointing the highest MRR exposure.
* **Tech Stack:** `SQL`, `Google BigQuery`, `Google Cloud Storage`, `Power BI`, `DAX`, `Star Schema`.

#### 💻 [Digital Health & Workplace Productivity Dashboard (Power BI)](https://github.com/renatosaldiviap/BI_Dashboard_Salud_Bienestar)
* **Problem:** Assess the correlation between daily screen exposure, physical activity, stress levels, and productivity across 3,500 evaluated records.
* **Methodology & Architecture:** Built an interactive `Power BI` executive report with custom `DAX` KPIs (high-risk population percentage, screen-time thresholds, and cohort segmentation).
* **Outcome:** Highlighted critical screen-time thresholds linked to burnout and quantified the protective impact of regular physical activity.
* **Tech Stack:** `Power BI`, `DAX`, `Power Query`, `Data Visualization`.

#### 🎮 [Gaming Habits & Mental Health: Exploratory Data Analysis](https://github.com/renatosaldiviap/gaming-mental-health-data-analysis)
* **Problem:** Analyze behavioral and lifestyle dynamics of gamers to understand relationships between play time, sleep patterns, and mental health metrics.
* **Methodology:** Systematic statistical auditing, hypothesis testing, bivariate correlation analysis, and data cleaning across high-dimensional survey data.
* **Outcome:** Derived actionable conclusions regarding lifestyle independence and behavioral risk factors among frequent players.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Seaborn`, `Matplotlib`.
