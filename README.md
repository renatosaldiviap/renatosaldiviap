## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌧️ [Rainfall Prediction in Australia (End-to-End CRISP-DM Pipeline)](https://github.com/RenatoSaldivia/australia-weather-data-analysis)
- **Problem:** Predict next-day rainfall under severe class imbalance (~78% dry vs. ~22% rain) using over 140,000 meteorological records.
- **Methodology & Architecture:** Complete CRISP-DM cycle, addressing data leakage, building a leakage-free `ColumnTransformer` (median/mode imputation, standard scaling, and one-hot encoding), and deploying a unified Scikit-Learn `Pipeline`.
- **Outcome:** **LightGBM** champion model achieving **0.8843 ROC-AUC** and **78.65% Recall** on actual rainy days, serialized via `joblib` with an inference-ready interface.
- **Tech Stack:** `Python`, `Scikit-Learn`, `LightGBM`, `Pandas`, `Matplotlib`, `Seaborn`.

### 🎮 [Gaming Habits & Mental Health: Exploratory Data Analysis](https://github.com/RenatoSaldivia/gaming-mental-health-data-analysis)
- **Problem:** Analyze the behavioral and lifestyle dynamics of gamers to understand relationships between play time, sleep patterns, physical activity, and mental health metrics.
- **Methodology:** Systematic statistical auditing, hypothesis testing, bivariate correlation analysis, and data cleaning across high-dimensional survey data.
- **Outcome:** Derived actionable conclusions regarding lifestyle independence and behavioral risk factors among frequent players.
